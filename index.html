Common questions

How does swiping work?

Each card is a recorded vote from an official who represents you. Swipe right to support it or left to oppose it. Governor cards use Sign and Veto. You can skip a card or undo your last answer. Swiping is continuous; tap Results beside the vote counter to see how your officials voted on everything you’ve answered so far.

How is my alignment calculated?

An official’s score counts only the votes where you and that official both took a position. Skipped cards, absences and voice votes are left out. A “match” means your answer and the official’s recorded vote were the same.

How do I change my district?

Tap the location pill at the top of the Swipe tab and enter your street address. Floorline sends the address to the U.S. Census Geocoder to find your congressional and state legislative districts. You can also explore the sample district in Topeka, Kansas.

Where does the information come from?

Votes, bills and member rosters come from public records published by the U.S. House Clerk, the U.S. Senate, GovInfo and Congress.gov, the Federal Election Commission, the Kansas Legislature, Open States and Bill Commons. Every card links to the official record so you can check it yourself.

Why don’t I see votes for my state?

State coverage depends on when each legislature publishes member-level votes. If your state hasn’t published recent roll calls, you’ll still see votes from your members of Congress.

How do vote alerts work?

Turn them on with the bell on the Swipe tab. Floorline checks the public feeds in the background and sends a notification from your device when a new vote from your officials is recorded.

Does Floorline take sides?

No. Floorline shows recorded votes and plain-language summaries attributed to their sources. It doesn’t endorse candidates, parties or positions.
