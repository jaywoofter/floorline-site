Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

The short version

  • Floorline doesn’t require an account.
  • Your answers on votes stay on your iPhone.
  • Floorline has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn’t sell or share your information.
  • If you enter an address, it’s sent to the U.S. Census Geocoder only to find your districts.

This policy explains what the Floorline iPhone app (“Floorline”, “the app”) does with information. Floorline is published by Jaden Woofter (“we”).

Information stored on your device

Floorline keeps the following only in the app’s storage on your iPhone. We don’t receive it.

  • Your answers (support, oppose or skip) on each vote, and the alignment scores calculated from them.
  • The name you choose to enter, if any.
  • Your matched city and congressional and state legislative districts.
  • Copies of public records the app has downloaded, and the time of the last refresh.
  • An Open States API key, if you choose to add one. It’s stored in the iOS Keychain.

Deleting the app removes this information from your device.

Information sent to other services

Address lookup

When you enter a street address, Floorline sends it to the U.S. Census Bureau’s Geocoder to find your districts. Floorline saves the resulting city and districts, not your street address. The Census Bureau’s handling of the request is described in its own privacy policy.

Public records

To show votes, bills and officials, the app requests public data from government and civic data services: the U.S. House Clerk, the U.S. Senate, GovInfo, Congress.gov, the Federal Election Commission, the Kansas Legislature, Open States (Plural Policy) and Bill Commons. These requests identify things like a state, district, bill or official. They never include your answers or your street address. Official portraits are loaded from public image hosts. Like any website, these services may receive standard network information such as your IP address, under their own policies.

Bill explanations

Floorline downloads reviewed, plain-language explanations of recent bills from this website, which is hosted on GitHub Pages. The request is the same for everyone. It doesn’t include your answers, your address or your districts.

Optional state data relay

Some builds fetch state bills through a relay operated for Floorline. Each relay request contains only a state abbreviation and a page number.

On-device explanations

On devices that support Apple Intelligence, Floorline can write a plain-language explanation of a bill on your iPhone. The model receives only public information: the bill title, the motion and published legislative text.

Notifications

If you turn on vote alerts, your iPhone checks the public feeds and creates notifications locally. Floorline doesn’t use a push notification server.

Children

Floorline isn’t directed to children under 13 and doesn’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and its effective date.

Contact
